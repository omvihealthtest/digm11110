Our Story

OMVI was built by people who were tired of guessing.

Where it started

Between crash diets, inconsistent bathroom scales and food logs that never quite added up, we set out to build hardware and software that finally speak the same language.

What began as a frustration with guesswork turned into two connected devices — a body scale and a kitchen scale — designed from day one to feed the same app, the same profile, and the same picture of your health.

Designed by the people who use it

Every OMVI product is designed, tested and refined by our own team — so what you hold in your hands is exactly what we use ourselves, every day. If a feature doesn't earn its place in our own routine, it doesn't ship.

Small team, high standard

We're a small team based in Australia, shipping to Australia and New Zealand. That means every unit is checked by someone who genuinely cares whether it works — not just whether it shipped.

Know your Body.

Body Composition Insights, Food Intake. All directly to you.
