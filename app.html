The OMVI App

Every weigh-in and every meal, in one place. The OMVI app turns raw numbers from your scales into a daily calorie target that updates itself.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

Daily Calorie Target

Calculated using the Mifflin–St Jeor equation and recalculated automatically every time you weigh in — so your target always reflects where you actually are, not where you started.

Set a Goal, Get a Plan

Maintain weight, lose fat or build muscle — pick a goal and the app adjusts your daily targets and macro breakdown to match, with a live preview before you confirm.

Body Data, Diet Data, Health Tools

Three tabs, everything connected: body composition history, food and macro logging, and tools to manage your ongoing goals. Whatever either scale measures lands in the app in real time.

Know your Body.

Body Composition Insights, Food Intake. All directly to you.
