Help & Support

Answers to common questions about your OMVI scales, shipping, warranty and returns.

How do I connect my scale to the app?

Open the OMVI app, create a profile, then step on the body scale or place an item on the kitchen scale with Bluetooth enabled on your phone. The scale pairs automatically the first time it detects the app nearby.

Do multiple people in one house need separate scales?

No. Each scale supports multiple profiles, so everyone in the household can weigh in separately and the app keeps each person's history distinct.

What's covered under warranty?

Every OMVI Body Scale and Kitchen Scale is covered by a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects from the date of purchase. This covers faulty sensors, display issues and battery failure under normal use.

What's your returns policy?

If you're not satisfied, return your OMVI product within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be in original condition with packaging. Contact us to arrange a return before sending anything back.

Are OMVI devices certified?

Yes. Both the Body Scale and Kitchen Scale meet relevant electrical safety and Bluetooth certification standards for sale in New Zealand and Australia.

Still need a hand?

Our support team typically replies within one business day.
