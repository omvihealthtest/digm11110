OMVI's Body Composition Scale uses bioelectrical impedance analysis (BIA), which is not recommended for people with a pacemaker or other implanted electronic medical device. If you are pregnant or have a medical device, speak with your doctor before use.

Consistency creates clearer trends.

  1. Measure at roughly the same time each day
  2. Use the scale on a hard, flat surface
  3. Measure before eating or drinking where possible
  4. Use clean, dry hands and feet
  5. Hold the handle correctly
  6. Focus on trends rather than one isolated reading
